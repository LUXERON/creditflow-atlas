# CreditFlow Atlas implementation plan

## Phase 0 — validate

Establish baseline bank outcomes, customer needs, legal perimeter, reward funding, and stop conditions.

## Phase 1 — pilot build

Deliver one-bank/one-partner milestone rules, consent, entitlement ledger, settlement, audit, and cohort reporting.

## Phase 2 — controlled live pilot

Run a limited cohort with a control group for 6–12 months. Measure completion, harm, complaints, cost, partner incrementality, and savings retention.

## Phase 3 — multi-partner platform

Add campaign configuration, inventory adapters, automated reconciliation, experimentation, and partner onboarding controls.

## Phase 4 — scale

Add multi-bank tenancy, enterprise identity, managed database, regional policy packs, resilience testing, and contracted expansion.

## Execution loop

For every release: define hypothesis → configure controls → launch bounded cohort → measure outcome and harm → risk review → expand, revise, or stop.
