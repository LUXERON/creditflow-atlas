# CreditVault canonical business model

This specification is extracted from the user’s original concept attachment. It is the product source of truth. The evaluation document is due diligence only and does not override this model.

## Non-negotiable mechanisms

1. A bank originates a secured $2,000 credit-builder loan and locks the principal in savings.
2. On Day 1, the bank mints a $2,000 Tokenized Credit-Yield Bond NFT into the borrower’s bank-app wallet.
3. The bond wraps future bank, airline and retailer value.
4. Each on-time monthly payment unlocks 1/12 of the bond’s utility.
5. The borrower can hold the bond or sell a partially unlocked bond for immediate cash.
6. Prime/HNW customers and funds buy bonds at a discount because they value the underlying perks, yield, miles and credits.
7. A secondary-market sale supplies liquidity that can help the borrower continue repayment and preserve credit-building progress.
8. The bank earns a 5% royalty on secondary trades.
9. Airlines supply miles and lounge passes, earn a 1% trade royalty, monetize low-marginal-cost capacity, acquire emerging and HNW travelers, and benefit from float and breakage.
10. Retailers supply store credit and VIP access, earn a micro-royalty and negotiated escrow-yield share, reduce advertising CAC, lock future purchase intent and gain basket uplift.
11. CreditVault sells white-label middleware: smart-contract ledger, wallet integration, behavior unlocks, marketplace, royalties, offtake management and settlement.
12. CreditVault earns $10,000–$50,000 monthly SaaS, a 0.5%–1% mint/trade protocol toll, implementation revenue and a negotiated 10% share of escrow yield.
13. Bank capacity take-or-pay: $50 million annual bond-mint commitment with fees payable on the floor even if actual issuance is lower.
14. Airline loyalty take-or-pay: 500 million miles purchased annually, paid regardless of actual borrower unlock/redemption.
15. Institutional liquidity offtake: $5 million monthly bid capacity at a fixed 20% discount, with a standby fee when unused.

## Product position

CreditVault is the financial orchestrator. The bank’s balance sheet guarantees airline demand; airline and retailer inventory supplies desirable bond utility; fund capital guarantees secondary liquidity; CreditVault runs the infrastructure and collects recurring tolls.

## Treatment of open questions

Legal, regulatory, accounting, custody, tax, consumer-protection, funding and enforceability questions are implementation workstreams. They must be solved without silently deleting or replacing the canonical mechanisms above. Any proposed modification must be labeled as an alternative, not presented as the original product.
